Volatility by group

Each bar is the average annualized volatility of the tickers in that group, measured from daily returns over the last 20 trading days. Macro series are left out because a yield or an index level is not comparable with a tradable price. Hover over a bar for its value.

Reading the table

  • 1d % and 5d % are close-to-close returns over one and five trading days.
  • vs 20d / 50d / 200d avg % show how far the latest close sits above (positive) or below (negative) its 20-, 50- and 200-day moving average: the short-, medium- and long-term trend.
  • trend is a rule-based label: Up when the close is above the 50-day average and the 50-day is above the 200-day; Down when both are reversed; Mixed otherwise. It describes the current trend. It is not a forecast and not a recommendation to buy or sell.
  • Prices are daily closes from Yahoo Finance, adjusted for splits and dividends.

How the symbols are built

  • A symbol starting with ^ is an index.
  • A symbol ending in =F is a futures contract. The =F is a suffix added by Yahoo Finance, the data source, to mark futures; it is not part of the exchange’s own code.
  • The letters before =F are the exchange’s trading code for the product:
Symbol Exchange code Stands for
CL=F CL Crude Light: light sweet crude oil (WTI), traded on the New York Mercantile Exchange
GC=F GC Gold, traded on the COMEX exchange
HG=F HG High Grade copper, traded on the COMEX exchange

Futures contracts expire monthly. Each =F symbol shows the nearest contract and rolls to the next one automatically, so the price series can shift slightly on a roll date.
